## 1. Background

The Parties have discussed Partner's interest in supporting the
Intelligent Commerce Protocol ("ICP") as a Founding Member of the
Foundation contemplated by `governance/FOUNDATION-CHARTER.md` ("the
Charter"). This Letter sets forth the Parties' mutual understanding
of the contemplated relationship.

This Letter is non-binding except as set forth in §6 (Confidentiality)
and §7 (Exclusivity), and is intended to facilitate diligence and
preparation for a Definitive Membership Agreement.

## 2. Contemplated activities

Subject to Definitive Agreement, Partner intends to:

1. **Review and provide structured feedback on ICP-1.0-DRAFT** within
   [30] days of execution of this Letter. Feedback may be public or
   confidential at Partner's option.
2. **Designate one technical reviewer** to participate in the ICP Core
   Working Group, with a time commitment of approximately [4–8] hours
   per month during ICPIP review cycles.
3. **Designate one business representative** to participate in board
   formation discussions.
4. **Subject to Definitive Agreement, pay Founding Member dues** of
   $250,000 per year for the initial two-year term ($500,000 total),
   payable upon Foundation incorporation.
5. **Sign the standard ICP IP Policy** (§5 of the Charter): CC-BY-4.0
   for spec prose, Apache-2.0 for schemas/test-vectors/code, and a
   royalty-free patent grant on any patents reading on ICP
   implementation.

## 3. StateSet activities

In consideration of Partner's intent, StateSet will:

1. **Provide Partner unrestricted preview access** to the ICP-1.0 spec,
   conformance suite, reference implementations, and contract source.
2. **Make founding-team time available** for diligence calls (estimated
   [4] hours over the next [60] days).
3. **Refrain from announcing other founding members** in a way that
   characterizes Partner's intent without Partner's prior approval.
4. **Maintain Partner's Letter of Intent in confidence** until either
   (a) Partner consents to public announcement, or (b) Definitive
   Agreement is executed.

## 5. What this Letter is NOT

This Letter does **not**:

- Create any obligation to fund the Foundation, beyond the conditional
  dues commitment in §2 #4 that arises only upon Definitive Agreement.
- Transfer any IP, patents, trademarks, or assets between the Parties.
- Constitute an investment by either Party in the other.
- Grant either Party rights in the other's products, source code, or
  trade secrets, except as expressly stated in §3 #1 (preview access).
- Bind either Party to refrain from independent development of
  competing or adjacent specifications.
- Imply any commercial relationship beyond Foundation membership.

## 6. Confidentiality (BINDING)

For a period of 24 months from execution, each Party will hold in
confidence all non-public information shared by the other Party in
connection with this Letter, including:

- The existence and contents of this Letter.
- Spec previews shared before public ratification.
- Other parties' identities or interest levels.

Confidentiality does NOT apply to:

- Information already in the public domain.
- Information independently developed.
- Information lawfully received from a third party without restriction.
- Disclosure required by law or regulator with reasonable notice.

## 7. Exclusivity (BINDING, limited)

For a period of 90 days from execution of this Letter, neither Party
will solicit or accept any agreement with a third party that would
materially impede that Party's ability to enter into the Definitive
Agreement.

This exclusivity does NOT prevent:

- Operating either Party's existing business.
- Continuing other partnership discussions disclosed at execution.
- Routine commercial activities.

## 8. Termination

This Letter terminates at the earliest of:

- Execution of the Definitive Membership Agreement.
- 180 days from execution if no Definitive Agreement is reached.
- Written notice by either Party with 14 days' lead.

Confidentiality (§6) survives termination for the full 24 months.
Exclusivity (§7) terminates immediately upon Letter termination.
