# Operations

StateSet iCommerce standardizes core operational workflows across bindings.

## Orders and fulfillment

- Create orders, reserve inventory, and move through status transitions.
- Attach shipment tracking and update delivery events.

## Inventory

- Track balances per location.
- Reserve and confirm stock for checkout.
- Adjust inventory with audit trails.

## Returns and refunds

- Open RMAs, approve/reject, and issue refunds.
- Keep payment and return records in sync.

## Subscriptions and invoicing

- Manage plans, billing cycles, and recurring invoices.
- Combine with payments and analytics.

See `examples/workflows.md` for step-by-step sequences.
